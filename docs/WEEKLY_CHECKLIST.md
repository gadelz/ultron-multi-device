## Remember

**Consistency > Perfection**

- Post weekly, even if small
- Engage daily, even if 10 minutes
- Respond to everyone, even if late

**Quality > Quantity**

- One great article > 5 mediocre ones
- One helpful comment > 10 spammy posts
- One genuine connection > 100 followers

**Patience > Haste**

- Growth takes time
- Trust is built slowly
- Community can't be rushed
